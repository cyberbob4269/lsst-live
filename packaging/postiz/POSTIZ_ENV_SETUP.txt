=======================================================================

How to activate (Git Bash, from vera-terminal/packaging/postiz/):

    cp .env.example .env

Then open .env and make exactly these edits:

1. JWT_SECRET — replace the placeholder with this generated per-install secret:

   16825e330b913594962df9c0b4daaea568769a5dc53c9127ddc55b88337f4e24

2. X_API_KEY / X_API_SECRET — paste the key and secret from your X developer
   app (the same credentials you use in vera-home).

Everything else in .env.example (DATABASE_URL, REDIS_URL, the three localhost
URLs) stays as-is — it matches the vendored docker-compose.yml.

Then: Social view in Vera Terminal → Re-check → Start Postiz.
